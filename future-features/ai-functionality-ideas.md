# 🍽️ Smart Food & Drink AI Features

## Smart Automation & Alerts
- **Loyalty Program Integration**: Alert when you're close to earning rewards at frequently visited places
- **Happy Hour Hunter**: Learn your drink preferences and notify about happy hours at places you'd enjoy
- **Seasonal Menu Alerts**: "Your favorite restaurant just added their fall menu - you loved their seasonal items last year"

## Advanced Analytics & Insights
- **Taste Profile Mapping**: Build a flavor profile based on restaurant choices and ratings
- **Quality vs. Price Optimization**: "You rated cheap eats 4+ stars but expensive places 3 stars - adjust your budget?"
- **Food Trend Analysis**: "Bubble tea spending increased 300% - is this a permanent habit change?"

## Predictive & Recommendation Engine
- **Crave Prediction**: "You typically get coffee around 3pm on weekdays - want to set a budget alert?"
- **Restaurant Recommendations**: Suggest new places based on your rating patterns and preferred cuisines
- **Seasonal Spending Alerts**: "You spent 40% more on coffee shops last winter - want to prep a hot beverage budget?"

## Health & Wellness Integration
- **Cooking Incentive**: "You could save $150/month cooking 3 more meals at home - here are recipes matching your takeout preferences"

## Gamification & Challenges
- **Culinary Explorer**: Get points for trying new cuisines or restaurants
- **Budget Master**: Challenges like "Stay under $50 for lunch this week"
- **Local Legend**: Badge for supporting local businesses vs. chains
